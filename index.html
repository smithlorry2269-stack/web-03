Our story

A corner bakery built on slow mornings

It started with a single deck oven and a sourdough starter named Sylvia. Fifteen years on, we still mix by feel, ferment overnight, and pull everything from the oven before the neighbourhood wakes up.

Nothing here is par-baked or shipped in. We mill part of our own flour, laminate our croissants by hand, and frost every cake to order. If it isn’t good enough for our own kitchen table, it doesn’t make it to the case.

From scratch, every day

How your loaf gets made

No mixes, no shortcuts. Just flour, water, salt, time — and a crew that shows up in the dark.

🌾
Step 01

Mill & mix

We blend stone-milled and heritage flours, then mix by hand to feel the dough come together.

⏳
Step 02

Slow ferment

Everything rests 12–48 hours with our wild starter for flavour you can’t rush.

🔥
Step 03

Bake at dawn

Ovens fire at 5 a.m. so the case is full and warm the moment we unlock the door.

🛍️
Step 04

Sell out, repeat

When it’s gone, it’s gone. Tomorrow we do the whole thing over again.
